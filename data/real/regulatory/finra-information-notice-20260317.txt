WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
The venue charges the SEC Section 31 fee on every equity sale, so the project's
coding of a rate change must be checkable as text, not as a number typed into
config. The 2026-04-04 change ($0.00 -> $20.60 per $1,000,000) is what this
Notice announces; the excerpt below is the operative statement.

SUMMARY, quoted
  "Effective April 4, 2026, the Section 31 fee rate applicable to specified
   securities transactions on the exchanges and in the over-the-counter markets
   will increase from its current rate of $0.00 per million dollars in
   transactions to a new rate of $20.60 per million dollars in transactions."

DISCUSSION, quoted
  "On February 27, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) announced
   in Fee Rate Advisory for Fiscal Year 2026 that, effective April 4, 2026, the
   Section 31 fee rate ... will increase ... to a new rate of $20.60 per million
   dollars in transactions."
  "...For purposes of FINRA calculations, the 'charge date' is the trade date,
   not the settlement date. See 17 CFR 240.31(a)(3)."

USE IN CODE
sim/config.py SEC31_PER_MILLION carries ("2026-04-04", 20.60) with the same
effect; this excerpt is the stored statement a reviewer can check offline.
