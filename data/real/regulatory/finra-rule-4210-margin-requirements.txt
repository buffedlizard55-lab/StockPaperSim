WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
  4210 is the rule body that config.MarginConfig's pattern-day-trader threshold
  and the day-trade counter refer to. Paragraph (f)(8)(B) is the day-trading
  section; its text and the six counting examples are reproduced in
  finra-regulatory-notice-21-13.txt alongside this directory, which carries the
  interpretations that decide the arithmetic.

USED FOR
  * the $25,000 special maintenance minimum for pattern day traders, reported
    (not enforced) by config.MarginConfig.pdt_equity_threshold;
  * the definition of day trading that sim/portfolio.py::_count_day_trade
    implements, together with the "except for positions held overnight"
    carve-out that keeps @OvernightCarry_NO at zero intraday round trips.
