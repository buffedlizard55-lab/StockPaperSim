WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
  It is the rule-side statement that a firm borrowing a customer's securities
  has to tell the customer about dividends it will owe on them. The venue's
  short positions are borrowers, so a dividend going ex while a position is
  short is a payment the short owes, not a dividend the short misses. That is
  the manufactured dividend in sim/portfolio.py::Account.settle_dividend and
  irregularity IR-31.

QUOTED, Rule 4330(b)(2)(B)(ii), which lists what the borrower must disclose to
the lending customer; item (g) in full:

  "g. potential tax implications, including payments deemed cash-in-lieu of
  dividend paid on securities while on loan; and"

Also quoted, 4330(b)(2)(B)(ii)(f), which is why the venue models a borrow fee at
all and treats availability as conditional:

  "f. that the securities may be 'hard-to-borrow' because of short-selling or
  may be used to satisfy delivery requirements resulting from short sales;"

NOTE ON THE PREVIOUS CITATION
  IR-31 as first drafted linked "finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/4337".
  That URL returns 404: the securities-lending customer-protection rule is
  4330, not 4337 (4337 does not exist in the current rulebook). Recorded as
  IR-33 rather than quietly corrected, because a citation that looks right and
  is wrong is the failure mode this project is trying to surface.
