WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
  config.FINRA_TAF_PER_SHARE and config.FINRA_TAF_MAX_PER_TRADE put a dated
  fee schedule on every sell a participant makes. Until 2026-09-18 those
  numbers were SECONDARY - corroborated by two broker fee schedules because
  FINRA's own rate table was believed to live in a PDF this environment could
  not read. It does not: the ruleboard renders the rate paragraph as HTML, and
  this excerpt pins the schedule to it. Closes IR-05.

QUOTED (paragraph (b)(3), "Fee Rates", footnote *)
  "Trading Activity Fee rates are as follows: Each member shall pay to FINRA:
  (1) $0.000195 per share for each sale of a covered equity security, with a
  maximum charge of $9.79 per trade; (2) $0.00329 per contract for each sale
  of an option; (3) $0.000135 per contract for each round turn transaction of
  a security future, provided there is a minimum charge of $0.016 per round
  turn transaction; (4) $0.00124 per bond for each sale of a covered
  TRACE-Eligible Security (other than an Asset-Backed Security) and/or
  municipal security, with a maximum charge of $1.24 per trade; and
  (5) $0.00000124 times the value, as reported to TRACE, of a sale of an
  Asset-Backed Security, with a maximum charge of $1.24 per trade. In
  addition, if the execution price for a covered security is less than the
  Trading Activity Fee rate ($0.000195 for covered equity securities, $0.00329
  for covered option contracts, or $0.016 for a security future) on a per
  share, per contract, or round turn transaction basis then no fee will be
  assessed."

  Paragraph (b)(1) defines the covered securities and (b)(2) lists thirteen
  exemptions (registration-statement securities other than OCC options,
  tender/exchange offers, warrant exercises, transactions executed outside the
  United States, exchange market-maker proprietary transactions and so on).
  Equity share volume is subparagraph (b)(3)(A).

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  Establishes, for the version in force for every 2026 session of Seasons 1
  and 2: the equity rate is $0.000195 per share, the per-trade maximum is
  $9.79, and the charge is levied on each SALE of a covered equity security.
  Two details that were previously guessed:

  * There is NO minimum charge on equity sales. The $0.01 floor quoted by
    several brokers is their own pass-through convention; Schedule A sets a
    maximum only for equities. sim/microstructure.py charges
    min(qty * rate, cap), which is what the rule says.
  * A sale whose execution price is below the per-share rate is not assessed
    at all. At this universe's prices ($9 to $670) the condition cannot bind,
    and it is not implemented; it is recorded here so that a future season
    trading sub-penny names does not inherit the gap silently.

  Does NOT establish the 2025 rate: this page is the 2026 version and the
  versions tab is not reachable as a stable URL. The 2025 numbers
  ($0.000166/share, $8.30 cap) are pinned by the SEC's publication of
  SR-FINRA-2024-019, excerpted in sec-release-34-101696-taf-rate-schedule.txt
  alongside this file, whose rate table carries a "2025 (no change)" column.
