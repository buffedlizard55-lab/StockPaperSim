        "Trading Activity Fee"
Publisher: FINRA
Retrieved: 2026-09-17

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
  config.FINRA_TAF_PER_SHARE and FINRA_TAF_MAX_PER_TRADE put a dated
  fee schedule on every sell a participant makes. This is the only
  FINRA-published page about that fee that this environment could read.

QUOTED
  "Details of the Trading Activity Fee - including the securities it applies
  to, transactions that are exempt from the fee and the fee rates - are in
  Section 1 of Schedule A to FINRA's By-Laws."

  "Effective July 1, 2026, FINRA will migrate the current TAF system to the
  E-Bill platform to streamline reporting and enhance system functionality."

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  It establishes that the TAF is levied on SALES of covered equity securities
  and that the rate table lives in Schedule A, which is a PDF this environment
  could not read. So the numbers in config.FINRA_TAF_PER_SHARE (0.000166/share
  to 2025-12-31, 0.000195/share from 2026-01-01) and the per-trade cap
  (8.30 then 9.79) remain status SECONDARY in the register: corroborated by
  two independent broker fee schedules, not verified against the primary.
  Recorded as IR-05; the 2026 migration is recorded as IR-33 context.
