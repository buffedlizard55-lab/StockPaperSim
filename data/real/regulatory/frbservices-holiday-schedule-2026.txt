WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
Settlement gates must not be guessed from weekday patterns. This schedule is
the publisher's own list of the days the Federal Reserve Banks (and therefore
Fedwire) are closed, so sim/venue_admin.settlement_date() derives T+1 from it
and the rows below remain checkable offline after the page moves.

LINK-ROT NOTE
The previous canonical path https://www.frbservices.org/resources/holidays
returned a 404 on 2026-09-19; this live path is now cited everywhere (IR-73).

TABLE CONTENT (2026 column), holiday name then observed date
  New Year's Day:        January 1
  Martin Luther King Jr. Day: January 19
  Washington's Birthday: February 16
  Memorial Day:          May 25       (per the FedACH table: processing ends May 23, resumes May 25 evening)
  Juneteenth National Independence Day: June 19 (FedACH processing ends June 18 11:30pm ET)
  Independence Day:      July 4       (holiday falls on a Saturday)
  Labor Day:             September 7
  Columbus Day:          October 12
  Veterans Day:          November 11
  Thanksgiving Day:      November 26
  Christmas Day:         December 25

SATURDAY/SUNDAY NOTES, quoted verbatim
  "*For holidays falling on Saturday, Federal Reserve Banks and Branches will be
    open the preceding Friday."
  "**For holidays falling on Sunday, Federal Reserve Banks and Branches will be
    closed the following Monday."

CONSEQUENCE USED IN CODE
2026-07-04 (Independence Day) falls on a Saturday, so 2026-07-03 is OPEN for
Fedwire/settlement even though the NYSE calendar closes (observed holiday) that
Friday. Good Friday 2026-04-03 is not a Federal holiday: settlement runs while
markets are closed. Both were cross-checked against the FedACH 2026 processing
table on the same page.
