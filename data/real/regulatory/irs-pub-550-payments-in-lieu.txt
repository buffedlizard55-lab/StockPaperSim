WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
  Corroboration, from the tax side, of the direction of the cash flow the venue
  was missing before IR-31 was fixed: the SHORT side pays.

SUBSTANCE, as published in Pub. 550 chapter 4 "Short Sales" and chapter 1
"Investment Income and Expenses":

  * A taxpayer who borrows stock to make a short sale "may have to remit to the
    lender payments in lieu of the dividends distributed while you maintain your
    short position."
  * Those payments in lieu are reportable as ordinary income by the recipient
    lender, and are not qualified dividends; the payer's deduction is limited by
    the holding-period rules (a short sale closed within 45 days cannot deduct
    the payment at all, it adjusts basis instead).

CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS SIMULATION
  A short position that spans an ex-date owes the dividend. Before the fix the
  venue let the short keep the ex-date price decline without charging it, which
  is not a simplification but a transfer of wealth from the (unmodelled) lender
  to the short seller. sim/portfolio.py now charges
  abs(entitled_qty at open) * per_share as a dividend_in_lieu carry event.
