WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
A scheduler must not trade through a regulatory halt, and "no halts today" must
be backed by the authority's own document, not inferred. Keeping the observed
document structure here lets reviewers confirm the parser matches what the
publisher actually serves.

OBSERVED STRUCTURE (from the live document)
- Root element: <rss version="2.0"> with one <channel>. Channel titles include
  "NASDAQTrader.com" and "NASDAQ Trade Halts".
- One <item> per halt. <title> is the issue symbol; <pubDate> the publication
  stamp (e.g. "Fri, 18 Sep 2026 04:00:00 GMT").
- <description> is a CDATA section containing an HTML table. Its header row
  names the columns in this fixed order (quoted verbatim):
  "Halt Date | Halt Time | Issue Symbol | Issue Name | Market | Reason Code |
   Pause Threshold Price | Resumption Date | Resumption Quote Time |
   Resumption Trade Time"
- Example live rows on 2026-09-19 (values are the publisher's, kept as proof of
  the column order): symbol "UZX" with reason code "T1" and empty resumption
  fields; older rows "TEST-ONLY" free text is NOT used in fixtures - tests use
  invented symbols against this real structure.
- Empty resumption cells mean the halt has no published resumption yet; that is
  what halted_symbols() reports as currently halted.
