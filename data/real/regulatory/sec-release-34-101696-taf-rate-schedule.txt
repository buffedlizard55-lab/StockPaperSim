WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
  The 2025 half of the Trade Activity Fee schedule in
  config.FINRA_TAF_PER_SHARE ("2025-01-01", 0.000166) and
  config.FINRA_TAF_MAX_PER_TRADE ("2025-01-01", 8.30). FINRA's Schedule A page
  is served as the current version only - the 2026 one - and Season 1's
  sessions from 2025-09-17 to 2025-12-31 are costed at the 2025 rate, so the
  2025 number needs a primary citation of its own. This is the Commission's
  publication of the FINRA filing that set it, and its rate table carries a
  "2025 (no change)" column beside the 2026 column. Closes IR-05 with
  finra-schedule-a-section-1-taf.txt.

QUOTED (Section II.A.1, "Trading Activity Fee", printed page 93714)
  "The current TAF rates, which reflect the fee adjustments made from 2022
  through 2024, are:
  (1) $0.000166 per share for each sale of a covered equity security, with a
  maximum charge of $8.30 per trade;
  (2) $0.00279 per contract for each sale of an option;
  (3) $0.00011 per contract for each round turn transaction of a security

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  Establishes the 2025 rate ($0.000166/share, $8.30 maximum) from the
  Commission's own publication of the filing that set it, and corroborates the
  2026 rate ($0.000195/share, $9.79 maximum) that Schedule A states as the
  operative 2026 version. The two documents agree to the digit, which is why
  config's schedule is unchanged by this verification: only its evidence
  label moved.

  It also establishes what the schedule is NOT: a filing that phases rates in
  through 2029 (2027 $0.000232/$11.61, 2028 $0.000240/$12.05, 2029
  $0.000249/$12.50). Both published seasons end 2026-09-16, so none of those
  later steps apply - but a future season crossing 2027-01-01 would need them,
  and this file is where the numbers for that are already stored.
