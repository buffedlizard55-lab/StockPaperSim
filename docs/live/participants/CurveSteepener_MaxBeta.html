The rule as it ran

Inputs: DGS10, DGS3MO

Sizing: up to 1.9x gross · leverage: 1.8-1.9x gross · cadence: every session, executed the next session · horizon: weeks to months

Entry rules

  • spread = DGS10 - DGS3MO, and its 21-session change
  • Change > +10bp -> target JPM 0.9x, XOM 0.9x (cyclicals)
  • Change < -10bp -> target TLT 1.9x (duration)
  • Otherwise hold 1.0x SPY

Exit rules

  • Daily re-evaluation of the same two official series

Evidence base

  • Regulation T sets initial margin at 50% and FINRA 4210 sets the maintenance floor, so leverage is a rule-bound quantity here — Federal Reserve Board Reg T; FINRA Rule 4210 [FETCHED-VERIFIED]
  • The term spread is a monitored recession/cycle indicator published by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis — FRED series T10Y3M / DGS10 / DGS3MO [FETCHED-VERIFIED]

Declared failure modes

  • A 21-session change in a slow spread is mostly noise
  • Two single names (JPM, XOM) carry idiosyncratic risk that dominates a 10bp curve move
  • The 2024-2026 collected window contains an inverted curve, so the steepening branch may simply never fire
