Exit rules

  • Daily recomputation of the same eight signs

Evidence base

  • Stacking many weak, correlated signals is a common retail construction and a standard placebo in the factor literature: the combination inherits the components' correlation and adds no independent information — this project's own placebo participant [SELF-REFERENCE]
  • Regulation T sets initial margin at 50% and FINRA 4210 sets the maintenance floor, so leverage is a rule-bound quantity here — Federal Reserve Board Reg T; FINRA Rule 4210 [FETCHED-VERIFIED]

Declared failure modes

  • Eight correlated financial series do not make eight independent votes
  • A sign is the least informative statistic available: it throws away every magnitude
  • Stacked signals dilute whichever one is actually predictive

The Live Book is a legacy forward-style research simulation, not an independently timestamped forward test or a live market feed. Signals, the benchmark, the trading calendar and the financing rate are official, free and publicly available; the executable bars are the collected Yahoo research files marked SECONDARY, so this is not an official-price competition and every page says what share of traded notional used an official reference price.
