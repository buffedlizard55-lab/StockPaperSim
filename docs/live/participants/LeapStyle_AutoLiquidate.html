The rule as it ran

Inputs: NASDAQCOM, VIXCLS

Sizing: 1.95x gross in one instrument · leverage: 1.95x gross · cadence: every session, executed the next session · horizon: days to weeks

Entry rules

  • Rank SPY, QQQ, IWM, GLD, TLT, NVDA, TSLA by 42-session return
  • Target the leader at 1.95x equity whenever the leader's return is positive, measured on the official Nasdaq/VIX regime read
  • When the leader's 42-session return is negative, target 0.6x TLT

Exit rules

  • Rotate on rank change
  • Flatten everything on the final session of the competition window

Evidence base

  • The Leap's published rules: total-return ranking, maximum leverage available to the account, all positions closed at the close of the competition — TradingView The Leap - December 2025 rules [FETCHED-VERIFIED]
  • Regulation T sets initial margin at 50% and FINRA 4210 sets the maintenance floor, so leverage is a rule-bound quantity here — Federal Reserve Board Reg T; FINRA Rule 4210 [FETCHED-VERIFIED]

Declared failure modes

  • Concentration into the fastest-trailing asset is the classic performance-chasing trade and it is documented to mean-revert
  • A one-session execution delay on a rank-rotation rule is expensive
  • The venue has no futures, so the leverage the real contest permits is approximated by a 2:1 equity margin account

The Live Book is a legacy forward-style research simulation, not an independently timestamped forward test or a live market feed. A strategy writes an intent after the close of one session and the venue settles it against a later verified daily bar through the same microstructure model the competition uses. Signals, the benchmark, the trading calendar and the financing rate are official, free and publicly available; the executable bars are the collected Yahoo research files marked SECONDARY, so this is not an official-price competition and every page says what share of traded notional used an official reference price. Nothing here is investment advice.
