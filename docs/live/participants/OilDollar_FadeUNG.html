The rule as it ran

Inputs: DCOILWTICO, DTWEXBGS

Sizing: up to 1.8x gross · leverage: 1.5-1.8x gross · cadence: every session, executed the next session · horizon: weeks

Entry rules

  • 21-session return of official DCOILWTICO and of official DTWEXBGS
  • Oil up and dollar down -> target XOM 1.0x plus UNG 0.8x
  • Oil down and dollar up -> target short UNG 1.0x
  • Otherwise hold 0.5x XOM

Exit rules

  • Daily re-evaluation; no stop

Evidence base

  • Regulation T sets initial margin at 50% and FINRA 4210 sets the maintenance floor, so leverage is a rule-bound quantity here — Federal Reserve Board Reg T; FINRA Rule 4210 [FETCHED-VERIFIED]
  • The Federal Reserve publishes the nominal broad dollar index the signal reads — FRED series DTWEXBGS (H.10 release) [FETCHED-VERIFIED]

Declared failure modes

  • UNG tracks front-month natural-gas futures with documented roll decay, so a correct gas call can still lose money through the wrapper
  • WTI spot and energy-equity earnings are not the same series
  • A two-condition macro filter fires rarely and can sit flat for months
