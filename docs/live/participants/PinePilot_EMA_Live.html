Entry rules

  • EMA(9) crossing above EMA(21) on QQQ -> target 1.5x equity

Exit rules

Evidence base

  • Time-series momentum - an asset's own past 12-month return predicts its next-month return across asset classes — Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen (2012), Journal of Financial Economics 104(2) [KNOWN-NOT-FETCHED]

Declared failure modes

  • A 9/21 EMA cross on a daily bar is a textbook whipsaw generator
  • Taking the whole position on the cross means the entry price is always the worst one of the move
  • No filter means it trades every chop

The Live Book is a forward test, not a live market feed. A strategy writes an intent after the close of one session and the venue settles it against a later verified daily bar through the same microstructure model the competition uses. Signals, the benchmark, the trading calendar and the financing rate are official, free and publicly available; the executable bars are the collected Yahoo research files marked SECONDARY, so this is not an official-price competition and every page says what share of traded notional used an official reference price.
