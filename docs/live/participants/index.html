The Live Book is a forward test, not a live market feed. A strategy writes an intent after the close of one session and the venue settles it against a later verified daily bar through the same microstructure model the competition uses. Signals, the benchmark, the trading calendar and the financing rate are official, free and publicly available; the executable bars are the collected Yahoo research files marked SECONDARY, so this is not an official-price competition and every page says what share of traded notional used an official reference price. Nothing here is investment advice.
