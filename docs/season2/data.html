What is real and what is still modelled

  • Real: every open, high, low, close and volume the strategies traded on (vendor files, cross-checked), the S&P 500 and VIX paths (FRED), the yield curve (FRED), FDA decision dates (openFDA), MLB finals (MLB StatsAPI), weather observations (NOAA/NCEI), Form 4 insider transactions where the SEC collection succeeded, dividends and splits (vendor event feed), and the SEC/FINRA fee schedules.
  • Modelled and declared: the intraday path inside each real daily bar; the venue's quoted spreads, displayed depth and market-making; the square-root impact model; the participation cap; borrow fees; and the assumption that each participant's order does not move the tape for anyone else. These are the same models Season 1 used and they are flagged in the irregularity register (IR-40 to IR-46).
  • Not retrieved: a consolidated-tape (SIP) history, which would be the only way to make the intraday path real rather than modelled; the SEC Form 4 collection if its request failed (the manifest says which); and any archived NFL/NBA injury series.

Season 2 trades real, collected daily prices (Yahoo Finance chart API files under data/real/prices/, cross-checked against the Nasdaq quote API and the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis' SP500 series) and real dated events (SEC EDGAR Form 4, FDA openFDA, MLB StatsAPI, NOAA/NCEI, FRED, Kalshi). The intraday path inside each real daily bar is still modelled, the venue is still modelled, and dividends come from a vendor event feed rather than the issuer - all three are declared in the irregularity register and on the data custody page. Nothing here is investment advice.
