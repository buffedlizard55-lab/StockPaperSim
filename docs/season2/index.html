How Season 2 differs from Season 1

Season 1 traded a calibrated replay: real S&P 500 and VIX path, fifteen synthesised single names. Season 2 trades the real daily bars of 24 instruments from files in this repository, with real volumes, real dividends and real event dates. What remains modelled is stated on every page: the intraday path inside each real bar, the venue's depth and impact, and execution costs.

Because real prices are a single realisation, there is no synthetic-seed robustness panel in Season 2. Instead the same real prices are re-run under two stress assumptions — doubled execution costs and halved available liquidity — and every participant's number is published under all three.
