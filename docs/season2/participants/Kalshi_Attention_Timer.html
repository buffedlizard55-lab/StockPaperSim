Mandate (as declared before the season)

@Kalshi_Attention_Timer (Kalshi Attention Timer) ran the "event attention proxy" archetype. Declared thesis: The Kalshi venue file records settled temperature and gasoline contracts with their volumes. Trading volume in those contracts is real-money attention on weather and fuel, and the participant holds the two linked ETFs while that attention is elevated. Entry: Sum the traded volume of settled Kalshi contracts closed in the trailing 30 days (KXHIGHNY, KXAAAGASM); Hold UNG at 100% and XLU at 60% of equity when the trailing volume is above its trailing median. Exit: Flatten when volume falls below the median; No stop loss. Sizing: 160% gross across two instruments. Leverage: 1.6x gross. Cadence: weekly, horizon weeks. Aggression 4/5. This participant was built to maximise return, not to manage risk: Uses an exchange's own data with no hedge.

Outcome (as measured after the season)

Outcome: -1.98% over 251 sessions - -1,983 USD on 100,000 of paper capital, ending at 98,017. Verdict: lost money.

Why: the style premia that were actually live

The momentum premium it is built to harvest was live (+22.09%) and the account STILL lost 1.98% - so the loss came from implementation, timing or leverage, not from a missing signal. The beta premium it is built to harvest was live (+8.32%) and the account STILL lost 1.98% - so the loss came from implementation, timing or leverage, not from a missing signal.

Why: attribution of the return

Of the -1.98% total, about -0.83pp is explained by carrying beta -0.06 against a +14.41% market (R^2 0.01); the residual -1.16pp is everything else - selection, timing, leverage and cost. UNG contributed +2,024 USD (-102% of net P&L) across 5 closed trades; XLU was the biggest drag at -4,007 USD.

Why: what execution cost

Execution cost 370 USD (0.37% of starting capital, 4.6 bp per fill, 10.4 bp of the 357,010 USD traded). 79% of that was spread/depth/impact and 21% was explicit fees and rebates.

Why: trade-level behaviour

14 closed round trips, 28.6% winners, profit factor 0.51, average winner +522 vs average loser -407 USD, median holding 18 sessions. Time in market, measured without double counting: 32 sessions held at least one position (the per-tranche holding periods above sum to 253 sessions because tranches of the same average-cost lot share its entry date, so they overlap and must not be added together). Single worst trade: XLU long 2026-08-03 to 2026-09-16, -3,819 USD.

What the return-maximising mandate cost in drawdown

Worst peak-to-trough drawdown -6.22% (-9.10% for the index), trough on 2026-08-06, recovered 2026-08-26. Average gross exposure 20% of starting capital, peak leverage 1.62x. Daily returns were -1.09 skewed with excess kurtosis +19.69; historical 95% VaR -0.36%/day with CVaR -1.69%/day. Worst single session -4.20%, best +3.40%.
