Mandate (as declared before the season)

@LeapMaxLever_Momentum (Leap Max-Leverage Momentum) ran the "levered beta" archetype. Declared thesis: The Leap ranks on total return with all positions liquidated at the end of the contest. Under that payoff the best rank-seeking response is to lever the asset with the strongest trailing return and hold it to the bell - the same rule is tested here on SPY, QQQ and TLT. Entry: Rank SPY, QQQ and TLT by 63-session return, skipping the last 5 sessions; Hold the leader at 190% of equity. Exit: Rotate at the start of each month; Auto-liquidation at the competition end is enforced by the engine for every participant. Sizing: 190% of equity, single name. Leverage: 1.9x gross. Cadence: monthly, horizon 1-3 months. Aggression 5/5. This participant was built to maximise return, not to manage risk: Directly maximises the contest's own objective function.

Outcome (as measured after the season)

Outcome: +38.13% over 251 sessions - +38,135 USD on 100,000 of paper capital, ending at 138,135. Verdict: beat the market.

Why: the style premia that were actually live

The beta premium this participant is built to harvest was live (+8.32% over the season) and the account made money - a tailwind that cannot be separated from skill on one path. The market premium this participant is built to harvest was live (+14.30% over the season) and the account made money - a tailwind that cannot be separated from skill on one path.

Why: attribution of the return

Of the +38.13% total, about +39.41pp is explained by carrying beta 2.73 against a +14.41% market (R^2 0.86); the residual -1.28pp is everything else - selection, timing, leverage and cost. QQQ contributed +37,079 USD (97% of net P&L) across 4 closed trades; QQQ was the biggest drag at +37,079 USD.

Why: what execution cost

Execution cost 16 USD (0.02% of starting capital, 0.2 bp per fill, 0.3 bp of the 516,610 USD traded). 49% of that was spread/depth/impact and 51% was explicit fees and rebates.

Why: trade-level behaviour

4 closed round trips, 100.0% winners, profit factor no losing trades, average winner +9,270 vs average loser +0 USD, median holding 208 sessions. Time in market, measured without double counting: 251 sessions held at least one position (the per-tranche holding periods above sum to 740 sessions because tranches of the same average-cost lot share its entry date, so they overlap and must not be added together). Single worst trade: QQQ long 2025-09-17 to 2026-01-02, +160 USD.
