Mandate (as declared before the season)

@MLB_Attention_Momo (MLB Attention Momentum) ran the "event attention proxy" archetype. Declared thesis: Betting handle follows attention, and attention follows a dense slate of games. The participant holds sportsbook and sports-data equities while the trailing week's MLB calendar is busier than its trailing median. Entry: Count MLB finals in the trailing 7 calendar days from the official StatsAPI schedule file; When the count is above the trailing-60-session median, hold DKNG, FLUT and PENN at 60% of equity each. Exit: Flatten when the count falls below the median; No stop loss. Sizing: 60% per name, three names = 1.8x gross. Leverage: 1.8x gross. Cadence: weekly check, horizon weeks. Aggression 4/5. This participant was built to maximise return, not to manage risk: Concentrated basket with no hedge and no stop.

Outcome (as measured after the season)

Outcome: +23.85% over 251 sessions - +23,849 USD on 100,000 of paper capital, ending at 123,849. Verdict: beat the market.

Why: the style premia that were actually live

The momentum premium this participant is built to harvest was live (+22.09% over the season) and the account made money - a tailwind that cannot be separated from skill on one path. The beta premium this participant is built to harvest was live (+8.32% over the season) and the account made money - a tailwind that cannot be separated from skill on one path.

Why: attribution of the return

Of the +23.85% total, about +5.25pp is explained by carrying beta 0.36 against a +14.41% market (R^2 0.01); the residual +18.60pp is everything else - selection, timing, leverage and cost. DKNG contributed +15,818 USD (66% of net P&L) across 37 closed trades; FLUT was the biggest drag at -4,133 USD.

Why: what execution cost

Execution cost 2,591 USD (2.59% of starting capital, 4.1 bp per fill, 5.8 bp of the 4,428,686 USD traded). 80% of that was spread/depth/impact and 20% was explicit fees and rebates.

Why: trade-level behaviour

105 closed round trips, 52.4% winners, profit factor 1.37, average winner +1,621 vs average loser -1,306 USD, median holding 16 sessions. Time in market, measured without double counting: 120 sessions held at least one position (the per-tranche holding periods above sum to 2019 sessions because tranches of the same average-cost lot share its entry date, so they overlap and must not be added together). Single worst trade: PENN long 2026-07-29 to 2026-08-28, -13,865 USD.

What the return-maximising mandate cost in drawdown

Worst peak-to-trough drawdown -28.08% (-9.10% for the index), trough on 2026-09-01, never recovered. Average gross exposure 89% of starting capital, peak leverage 1.90x. Daily returns were +1.00 skewed with excess kurtosis +6.90; historical 95% VaR -4.58%/day with CVaR -6.82%/day. Worst single session -12.69%, best +14.21%.
