Mandate (as declared before the season)

@Weather_ColdSnap_Max (Weather Cold Snap Max) ran the "event attention proxy" archetype. Declared thesis: SFWeather's brief is a rain outlook, but the tradable temperature path is the same NOAA record: cold anomalies at the SF station are a heating-demand proxy, traded through natural gas and utilities. Entry: Count days in the trailing 10 with TMIN below the trailing 60-day mean minus one standard deviation (NCEI station USW00023272); When the count is at least 1, hold UNG at 110% and XLU at 60% of equity. Exit: Flatten when the count returns to zero; No stop loss. Sizing: 110% UNG + 60% XLU = 1.7x gross. Leverage: 1.7x gross. Cadence: daily, horizon days to weeks. Aggression 4/5. This participant was built to maximise return, not to manage risk: Two-instrument concentrated position with maximum size.

Outcome (as measured after the season)
