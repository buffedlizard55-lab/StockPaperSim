Declared rules (written before the run)

Entry:

  • Read DGS10 and DGS3MO from the collected FRED files
  • Steepening (21-day change positive) -> hold SPY and QQQ at 90% of equity each
  • Flattening -> hold TLT at 150% of equity

Exit:

  • The regime flip is the exit
  • No stop loss

Sizing: 180% gross equities or 150% single bond ETF · Leverage: 1.8x gross · Cadence: daily

Declared failure modes:

  • The curve is a slow signal; regime flips are rare and lagged
  • Bond ETFs carry duration risk that is not modelled beyond price
  • The same series is used by everyone, so the information is priced
