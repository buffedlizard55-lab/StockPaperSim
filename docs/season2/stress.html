Official-price eligibility: NOT ELIGIBLE

This committed run uses yahoo price files classed SECONDARY. It is reproducible research, not an official-price competition result. The strict runner uses the Nasdaq adapter and refuses to trade when official files, raw checksums, complete dates, OHLCV validation or redistribution permission are missing.

Audit flags: DATE_RANGE, DIVIDEND_DATA_NOT_VERIFIED, DIVIDEND_PROVENANCE_MISSING, MANIFEST_CHECKSUM_NOT_FOUND, MISSING_SESSIONS, NON_OFFICIAL_SOURCE_CLASS, REDISTRIBUTION_NOT_CONFIRMED. Run python3 -m sim.cli price-audit for the complete report; no Yahoo/Nasdaq agreement can override this gate.

Stress panel — the same collected path, a harder venue

The collected price path is one realisation: there is no synthetic-seed panel to average over the way Season 1 has one. What is still a modelling choice is the venue, so Season 2 re-trades the identical collected bars under two harder assumptions and publishes every participant's return under all three. A strategy whose edge survives both is not relying on a cheap spread.

Assumptions

  • primary — the venue as declared in sim/config.py: the tick-constrained spread model, the square-root impact model, a 10% participation cap of the collected session volume, and a zero-commission retail broker that still pays exchange and regulatory fees.
  • stress-costs2x — spread coefficient doubled, impact coefficient doubled, taker fee per share doubled, and a $1.00 commission per order (the price the referenced paper competitions charge).
  • stress-thinliquidity — displayed depth at the touch halved and the participation cap cut from 10% to 5% of the collected session volume.
