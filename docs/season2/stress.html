Stress panel — the same real prices, a harder venue

Real prices are one realisation: there is no synthetic-seed panel to average over the way Season 1 has one. What is still a modelling choice is the venue, so Season 2 re-trades the identical real bars under two harder assumptions and publishes every participant's return under all three. A strategy whose edge survives both is not relying on a cheap spread.

Assumptions

  • primary — the venue as declared in sim/config.py: the tick-constrained spread model, the square-root impact model, a 10% participation cap of the real session volume, and a zero-commission retail broker that still pays exchange and regulatory fees.
  • stress-costs2x — spread coefficient doubled, impact coefficient doubled, taker fee per share doubled, and a $1.00 commission per order (the price the referenced paper competitions charge).
  • stress-thinliquidity — displayed depth at the touch halved and the participation cap cut from 10% to 5% of the real session volume.
