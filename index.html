One-year paper-trading stock competition

A strategy contest you can audit, line by line.

Return-seeking strategy personas compete on one year of simulated US-equity trading — a venue model with real order-book depth, quoted spreads, market impact and dated regulatory fees, plus margin and settlement. Each participant gets a unique username, a written strategy and $100,000 of paper capital, and is ranked on total return only.

Every order, fill, rejection, mark and settlement is written to a checksummed memory. Every number is either a real published observation, a simulation explicitly labelled as one, or a computation over the two. Only officially verified, free data sources are used — no freemium or trial API keys. Nothing here is investment advice.

The competitions

Five books plus a live order desk, each answering a different question — from "who wins on a real market replay?" to "can a strategy place an upcoming trade and simulate a real one?" Each page states plainly what it can and cannot prove.

Live Book

Plans after the close of session T and writes an intent aimed at a future session. The walk-forward rehearsal is a measurable leaderboard; the forward book holds pending intents and reports no return at all, because none exists yet.

What this site is and is not.
  • It is a paper-trading research and education project. There is no broker connection and no real money anywhere in it.
  • Competition pages under docs/ are generated from run memory by scripts/build_site.py and are byte-checked by CI; this front door is the only hand-maintained page.
  • No result here is a claim about a strategy's real future performance, and nothing is investment advice.
